Lipschitz gradient

\[|f'(y)-f'(x)|\le L|y-x|.\]

The slope cannot change faster than \(L\).

The tangent becomes a quadratic envelope:

\[\begin{aligned}f(y)&\le f(x)+f'(x)(y-x)\\&\quad+\tfrac L2(y-x)^2,\\f(y)&\ge f(x)+f'(x)(y-x)\\&\quad-\tfrac L2(y-x)^2.\end{aligned}\]

Example: \(f(x)=\sin x+0.1x^2\).
Since \(|f''(x)|\le1.2\), choose \(L=1.2\).

In higher dimensions:
\(\|\nabla f(\mathbf y)-\nabla f(\mathbf x)\|_2\le L\|\mathbf y-\mathbf x\|_2\).
